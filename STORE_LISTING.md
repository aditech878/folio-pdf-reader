# Folio PDF Reader — listing draft

**Summary:** A focused reader for your PDFs

Read PDFs with room to think. Folio gives you a calm document view with
continuous pages, thumbnails, and a searchable contents sidebar.

Folio is free and open source under the GNU AGPL version 3 or later. If you
enjoy it, you can support development at
https://buymeacoffee.com/aadityabanwari.

Features:

- Search text across the document and jump between matches.
- Fit the page or its width, rotate pages, and switch to night colors.
- Open several PDFs in tabs and return to recent files.
- Copy selected text and print pages.
- Open documents locally without uploading them.
